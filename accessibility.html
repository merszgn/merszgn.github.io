Translations are easy accessible. There are sections that I have intentionally forced to stay in their original languages. Translation services via browser extensions should, ideally, be accurate enough. However, a mouthful can be lost in translation no matter how fancy your translator may be.

«Ōdī et amō. Quārē id faciam, fortasse requīris.

Nesciŏ, sed fierī sentiō et excrucior.»

Small screens are advised against. Although I put in my best effort to make the website as responsive as possible, small screens and mobile devices are not ideal for viewing. It should ultimately work fine, but it will not provide remotely the same experience that a moderate or large screen will.

Due to the innate nature of the website, I will not be working on a version to fit mobile devices. I had originally intended for it to be viewed on exclusively computers, and I plan on continuing the same path.
